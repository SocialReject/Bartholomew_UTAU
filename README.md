# Bartholomew_UTAU
UTAU Beast English Vocalist Prototype for Country and Folk

# ABOUT
This is a test voicebank of the beast country vocalist project Bartholomew

For a few years I've wanted to develop a singing synthesis voicebank that could theoretically be suitable for folk and country.

Recently I've started developing Bartholomew and created a prototype that can be used.

There is currently no license to this voicebank, it is for personal use only. I'm uploading this for hobbyists who would like a UTAU vocalist for less utilized genres.

Use the rendered vocals for use in Band in a Box or whatever music making software of your choice!

# VOCAL
Bartholomew is an American English vocalist with a Midwest accent. He was recorded mostly with a slightly high register, he's a little punchy but relaxed.

When I was testing him out, I found he can go from F3 to F4 pretty nicely, so his range is moderately large.

He was recorded in one pitch, more pitches are planned. He lacks 'dx' phonemes, so I recommend using 'd' phonemes with some edits in the timing.

There are some pronunciation issues, using available alternative phonemes is recommended. He can also glitch at times and his oto.ini is about 85% complete.

Another thing to note is that he was recorded with a low quality microphone not designed for studio recording. He can be low quality and I tried my best to process his samples to make them better.

# DEFINTIONS
"Beast" in this case is an umbrella term for a character with animalistic characteristics in general. Bartholomew is both a character that could appeal to furries and therians.

Hopefully it'll also appeal to anyone, you don't really have to use his character with the voicebank if you don't want to.
